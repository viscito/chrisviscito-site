About

The voice behind the mic

Chris Viscito is a voice over artist with a warm, versatile delivery that moves easily between straight-ahead commercial reads, measured long-form narration, and fully-realized character work. With experience in Radio News Broadcasting, Chris brings the same attention to intention and pacing that a director would ask for on set — just without the camera.

Studied Radio, TV, Film at the University of North Texas. Recent work includes product launch marketing, online learning content, and product demos. Comfortable taking live pickups and direction mid-session.

  • MicrophoneRode NT1 5th Gen
  • InterfaceScarlett Solo 3
  • RoomAcoustically treated home studio
  • Remote directionZoom · FaceTime
  • Based inSaint Charles, IL (Chicago area) · Works with clients worldwide
